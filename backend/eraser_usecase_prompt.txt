### 1. System Boundary
The system boundary is the "MediApp Platform". All use cases must exist inside this boundary.

### 2. Actors and Inheritance
Define the following Actors. Note the inheritance structure:
- **Guest / Unauthenticated User**: An actor outside the system who hasn't logged in.
- **User (Base Actor)**: The core authenticated user.
- **Patient**: Inherits from "User".
- **Doctor**: Inherits from "User".
- **Admin**: Inherits from "User".
- **Caregiver**: A role played by a "User" who manages a specific "Patient". Inherits from "User".
- **System**: An automated background actor (handles cron jobs, token expirations, automatic low-stock notifications).
