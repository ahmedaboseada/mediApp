User Functional Requirements
What a system must do from the end-user's perspective, detailing specific features, actions, and behaviors like login, search, or data processing needed for users to achieve their goals.

1. User Registration and Authentication
Account creation for Patients and Doctors, secure login and logout using email and password, password reset functionalities, and two-factor authentication (2FA) support.

2. Patient Profile Management
Complete profile with chronic conditions, allergies, active ingredients to avoid, emergency contacts, and payment method integration.

3. Doctor Profile and Verification
Submit medical license for verification, setup clinic location, define working days and hours, and manage consultation preferences (duration, type).

4. Appointment Booking and Scheduling
Search for doctors, filter by specialization, book online or in-person appointments, specify booking subjects (self or dependent), and handle booking payments.

5. Health and Medical Tracking
Log health measurements (blood pressure, blood sugar, weight, heart rate), track daily symptoms and severity, and monitor general wellbeing.

6. Medicine Management and Adherence
Add new medicines with specific frequencies and dosages, track medicine inventory and stock alerts, mark adherence (taken/missed), and submit daily feedback.

7. Caregiver Connection
Send caregiver requests to other users, accept or reject requests, and monitor dependent patient's health data and medicine adherence.

8. Communication and Chat
Initiate chat sessions between connected doctors and patients, send text messages, voice notes, and file attachments, and track message status.

9. Doctor Patient Management
Accept or reject patient connections, view complete patient medical files, add or modify patient prescriptions, and track audit logs for medical alterations.

10. Financial Management
Process payments for appointments, track doctor wallet balances, and submit withdrawal requests to bank accounts.

11. Admin Operations
Approve or reject doctor registrations, process withdrawal requests, and monitor system-wide audit logs.

System Functional Requirements (FR)
What a system must do, detailing specific behaviors, features, and functions like user authentication, data processing, calculations, and workflows.

1. User Management - Registration & Auth
- FR-1 Register Patient account with email, password, and first/last name.
- FR-2 Register Doctor account with email, password, and first/last name.
- FR-3 Send OTP via email upon registration for verification.
- FR-4 Verify email address by confirming OTP.
- FR-5 Support secure User login returning JWT authentication tokens.
- FR-6 Support secure User logout by blacklisting/revoking the session token.
- FR-7 Initiate password reset by requesting a password recovery email.
- FR-8 Update account password using the recovery OTP.
- FR-9 Request email address change generating a pendingEmail state.
- FR-10 Confirm new email address using OTP.
- FR-11 Toggle Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) for enhanced security.

2. Profile Management & Settings
- FR-12 Update basic profile information (gender, date of birth, phone number).
- FR-13 Upload, update, or remove profile picture.
- FR-14 Manage Notification Settings (Toggle Push Notifications, Toggle Email Notifications).
- FR-15 Manage Privacy Settings (Toggle Caregiver Access, Toggle Doctor Access).
- FR-16 Complete Patient Medical Profile (log blood type, chronic conditions, allergies, active ingredients to avoid).
- FR-17 Add, update, or delete Patient Emergency Contacts (Name, Phone, Relation, Contact Method).
- FR-18 Add, update, or delete Patient Bank Card details (Card Number, Expiry, Toggle Save option).
- FR-19 Complete Doctor Professional Profile (Specialization, License ID, Profile Summary).
- FR-20 Upload Doctor Medical License image for Admin verification.
- FR-21 Setup Doctor Clinic Location (Label, Address, Latitude, Longitude).
- FR-22 Define Doctor Working Schedule (Days of the week, Start time, End time).
- FR-23 Configure Doctor Consultation Preferences (Allowed Durations, Session Type, Max Daily Patients).

3. Booking & Scheduling Module
- FR-24 Fetch paginated list of approved Doctors with active schedules.
- FR-25 Filter Doctors by specialization, availability, and rating.
- FR-26 View detailed Doctor Profile including availability slots for a specific date.
- FR-27 Calculate and return valid available time slots based on Doctor's working hours and existing bookings.
- FR-28 Submit an Appointment Booking (Doctor ID, Date, Time, Duration, Type, Problem Description).
- FR-29 Specify Booking Target (Self or Dependent/Family Member with Age and Gender).
- FR-30 Validate Doctor's maximum daily patient limit before accepting new bookings.
- FR-31 Detect and prevent overlapping bookings or double-booking conflicts.
- FR-32 Process booking payment via Payment Gateway and update payment status (pending -> paid).
- FR-33 Generate unique secure Session Token for Online Consultations.
- FR-34 Update booking lifecycle statuses (upcoming, pending, completed, cancelled).
- FR-35 Allow Patient to cancel booking with a provided cancellation reason.
- FR-36 Allow Doctor to approve or reject pending booking requests.

4. Health Tracking Module
- FR-37 Record daily Health Measurements (Systolic BP, Diastolic BP, Heart Rate).
- FR-38 Record body weight and blood sugar measurements with notes.
- FR-39 Store and aggregate historical health measurement data for trend analysis.
- FR-40 Log daily Symptoms with customizable severity levels (none, mild, moderate, severe).
- FR-41 Provide daily graphical charts and dashboards for Patient Health progress.

5. Medicine & Prescription Module
- FR-42 Add new Medicine to a Patient's prescription (Name, Active Ingredient, Start/End Date).
- FR-43 Configure Medicine intake frequency (Daily time arrays, Weekly blocks, or specific explicit dates).
- FR-44 Manage Medicine Dosage details and usage instructions.
- FR-45 Track current Medicine Stock levels against a defined Left Threshold.
- FR-46 Toggle Medicine 'Critical Mode' and 'Is Chronic' flags.
- FR-47 Mark specific medicine doses as taken or missed for a given date/time.
- FR-48 Automatically increment/decrement Medicine Stock based on 'taken' status toggles.
- FR-49 Submit daily textual feedback and rating (low/medium/high) for medicine effectiveness.
- FR-50 Flag or log specific side effects experienced from a medication.
- FR-51 Send automated system alerts to Patient when medicine stock drops below the Left Threshold.

6. Caregiver Module
- FR-52 Create Caregiver Request linking a User to a Patient.
- FR-53 Define relationship type in Caregiver Request.
- FR-54 Accept or reject pending Caregiver Requests.
- FR-55 Grant approved Caregiver read-only access to Dependent's Health Measurements.
- FR-56 Grant approved Caregiver read-only access to Dependent's Medicine Adherence and Stock.
- FR-57 Revoke or terminate an active Caregiver connection.

7. Communication & Chat Module
- FR-58 Initiate chat sessions between a Patient and an approved connected Doctor.
- FR-59 Send and store real-time text messages in active Chat sessions.
- FR-60 Upload and send File Attachments (Images, PDFs) within a Chat session.
- FR-61 Upload and send Voice Notes with duration tracking in a Chat session.
- FR-62 Track and update message read receipts (unseen -> seen).
- FR-63 Fetch paginated message history for a specific Chat ID.

8. Doctor Operations & Medical Auditing
- FR-64 View a paginated list of all Patients connected to a Doctor.
- FR-65 Accept or reject Patient connection requests.
- FR-66 View full Medical History (allergies, symptoms, health measurements) of a connected Patient.
- FR-67 Doctor explicitly adds a new Medicine to a Patient's profile.
- FR-68 Doctor modifies an existing Medicine (dosage, frequency) in a Patient's profile.
- FR-69 Doctor deletes or stops an active Medicine in a Patient's profile.
- FR-70 Automatically generate an immutable Audit Log record when a Doctor alters a Patient's medical data.
- FR-71 Store previous and new values within the Audit Log for accountability.

9. Financials & Wallet Module
- FR-72 Retrieve and display Doctor's current Wallet Balance.
- FR-73 Automatically credit Doctor Wallet Balance upon the completion of a paid consultation.
- FR-74 Submit a Wallet Withdrawal Request (Amount, Bank Name, Account Number, Account Holder).
- FR-75 Validate requested withdrawal amount against available Wallet Balance.
- FR-76 View history of all past and pending Withdrawal Requests.

10. Admin & System Operations
- FR-77 Fetch list of unverified Doctors pending approval.
- FR-78 Admin approves or rejects Doctor registrations, updating their status flag.
- FR-79 Admin fetches list of pending Withdrawal Requests.
- FR-80 Admin processes Withdrawal Request, adds internal Admin Notes, and sets processed timestamp.
- FR-81 Fetch paginated System Audit Logs for administrative review and compliance checks.

11. Notifications Module
- FR-82 Generate and store in-app Notification records for booking updates.
- FR-83 Push real-time notifications to Doctors for new appointment requests.
- FR-84 Push real-time notifications to Patients for booking confirmations/cancellations.
- FR-85 Mark fetched notifications as read by the user.
- FR-86 Execute cron job to expire unused OTPs and Session Tokens automatically.
